Questions before you buy

Where do I buy?

All purchases are completed on Shopee, so you get Shopee's payment options, shipping and buyer protection. Every "Buy on Shopee" button opens the product in a new tab.

Are the products halal?

Yes. Wardah's products are halal certified by LPPOM MUI, Indonesia's national halal authority. Wardah was the first cosmetics brand in Indonesia to receive this certification.

Are the products organic?

The products themselves are not certified organic. The Superserum Mask's sheet is made from 100% organic cotton and is biodegradable, according to its packaging.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

The Superserum Mask and Mousse Moisturiser are dermatologically tested, and the Face Mist contains soothing allantoin. Everyone's skin is different, so patch-test each product on your inner arm for 24 hours first, and stop if you notice stinging or redness. The skin quiz has tips for sensitive skin.

Will it clog my pores in the humidity?

The Mousse Moisturiser is labelled non-comedogenic, meaning it's formulated not to clog pores. If your skin is oily, apply a thin layer and cleanse after heavy sweating.

Which order do I apply the products in?

Morning: Creamy Wash, Face Mist, Mousse Moisturiser, then DD Cream SPF 30 last. Night: Creamy Wash, Face Mist, Superserum Mask (2 to 3 times a week), then Mousse Moisturiser. See the routine guide.

How do I use my discount codes?

Redeem points in your rewards wallet to get a code, copy it, then enter it in the voucher field at Shopee checkout. Codes last 30 days, and you can use one per order.
