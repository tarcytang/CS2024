Vitamin C, the heart of C-Defense

When skin is exposed to UV and pollution, it produces free radicals. These cause oxidative stress, which is linked to premature ageing, dullness and pigmentation.1

Vitamin C is an antioxidant: it neutralises free radicals before they damage healthy skin cells.

  • Helps reduce oxidative stress
  • Supports brighter-looking, more radiant skin
  • Supports healthy collagen production
  • Helps protect skin from environmental damage

Antioxidants neutralise them

Antioxidants like vitamin C stabilise these molecules before they cause significant damage. That's why antioxidant skincare matters more as environmental stress rises.2

Hydration that holds

Transepidermal water loss (TEWL) is the moisture that escapes out through your skin. When it rises, the barrier weakens and skin feels dry, irritated and sensitive. A 2022 review of 15 studies found that every study on air pollution showed higher TEWL in people exposed to particulate matter or NO₂, while results for temperature and season were mixed.3

The Face Mist, Superserum Mask and Mousse Moisturiser help retain water in the skin, reduce moisture loss and support the barrier, so skin copes better with daily stress.

SPF 30 against UV damage

The DD Cream contains SPF 30. UV exposure is a major cause of premature skin ageing, including photoageing, hyperpigmentation, fine lines and loss of elasticity.1

Daily sun protection limits the cumulative effect of UV, making it an essential part of any climate-adaptive routine.
